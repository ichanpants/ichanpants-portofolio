BUG-01Diagonal movement is faster than straight movementMajor

Player report

“Running into corners feels like a speed boost. I can zigzag past the red orbs and they never catch me.”

Steps to reproduce

  1. Press “Reproduce in the build” below.
  2. Click the arena, then hold D and read Move speed in the sidebar.
  3. Release, then hold W and D together and read Move speed again.

Expected

Move speed is 220 px/s in all eight directions.

Actual

220 px/s straight, 311 px/s diagonal (1.41 times faster). The readout turns red.

Cause

Holding two direction keys builds the vector (1, 1), whose length is √2. The game applied it as-is instead of scaling it back to length 1.

Fix

 let mx = (inp.right ? 1 : 0) - (inp.left ? 1 : 0); let my = (inp.down ? 1 : 0) - (inp.up ? 1 : 0);+if (mx && my) { const len = Math.hypot(mx, my); mx /= len; my /= len; }

Pass criteria

  • Move speed reads 220 px/s in all eight directions.
  • Check BUG-01 passes: diagonal and straight distances match within 2%.
  • Dash speed is unchanged at 660 px/s.
BUG-02One orb touch ends the runCritical

Player report

“I barely clipped an orb and got game over instantly. I didn’t even see my lives go down.”

Steps to reproduce

  1. Press “Reproduce in the build” below.
  2. Turn on Slow motion (0.25×) so each frame is visible.
  3. Steer into an orb and watch Lives and Hit protection in the sidebar.

Expected

Lose exactly one life. The player blinks for 1.2 s and cannot be hurt during that time.

Actual

Lives go 3, 2, 1, 0 on three consecutive frames (0.05 s). Hit protection stays at None.

Cause

Collision runs on every fixed update, 60 times a second, and nothing records that the player was just hit. One overlap counts as many hits.

Fix

 if (overlapping(orb, player)) {+  if (p.invuln > 0) continue;   w.lives--;+  p.invuln = 1.2; // seconds of hit protection }

Pass criteria

  • First contact takes Lives from 3 to 2, and Hit protection counts down from 1.2 s.
  • The player blinks while protected.
  • Check BUG-02 passes: standing inside a stationary orb for 2.0 s leaves exactly 1 life.
BUG-03Dashing through a gem sometimes scores 20 instead of 10Minor

Player report

“My score jumps by 20 on some gems, but only when I dash. Walking over them is always 10.”

Steps to reproduce

  1. Press “Reproduce in the build” below.
  2. Walk onto a few gems and confirm Score rises by 10 each time.
  3. Dash through gems with Space ten or more times. Some pickups add 20, others add 10, depending on where the dash lands.

Expected

Every gem is worth exactly 10, however it is collected.

Actual

Some dash pickups add 20. It is intermittent, which is why it takes several dashes to see.

Cause

A dash moves the player in two sub-steps per frame so it cannot skip over small gems. The pickup check runs after each sub-step, but collected gems are only removed at the end of the frame. A gem in range at both sub-steps is scored twice.

Test design note

Because the bug is intermittent, the automated check places a gem 30 px ahead of the player. At that distance both sub-steps of the second dash frame overlap the gem, so the bug reproduces every time instead of by luck.

Fix

 for (const g of w.gems) {   if (!overlaps(p, g)) continue;+  if (taken.has(g)) continue;   w.score += g.value;+  taken.add(g); }

Pass criteria

  • Every gem adds exactly 10 across 20 dash pickups.
  • Check BUG-03 passes: score is 10 after one dash through a gem.
